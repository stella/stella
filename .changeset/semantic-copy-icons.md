Name the copy glyph by meaning: `CopyToClipboardIcon` (drawn only by `CopyButton`), `CopyFromMenuIcon` and `DuplicateIcon` replace the plain `CopyIcon` export. `CopyButton` takes a `labelClassName` for its visible label, and `useCopyConfirmation` exposes its in-place confirmation for copy controls that cannot be a glyph button.
