Add `DayCalendar` (`@stll/ui/day-calendar`): a vertical one-day calendar with caller-defined columns of time blocks (segments, merged `mixed` runs and entries in a matter or neutral tone), a 15, 30 or 60 minute grid, range selection by pointer drag or keyboard, a caller panel anchored beside the selected range, a now-line and locale-formatted hour labels. A `locked` column paints fixed placeholder bars behind a caller action and takes no blocks. `@stll/ui/day-calendar.logic` exports the day bounds, time-to-pixel mapping, snapping, overlap lanes and `mergeShortSegments`.
