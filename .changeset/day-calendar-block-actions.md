`DayCalendar` blocks take a `tooltip` of `{ type: "text", content }` or `{ type: "actions", content }`. An actions tooltip opens as a popover on hover, focus or press, so its buttons can be reached by pointer and keyboard, and stays open after a press inside it until it is dismissed; keys and presses inside it no longer start a range selection.
