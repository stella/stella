Add `TimeField` and `TimeFieldGroup` (`@stll/ui/time-field`): a wall-clock time to the minute as hour and minute selects in the locale's own clock and numerals. Fields in one `TimeFieldGroup` share a grid, so their labels, hours and minutes line up; in a narrow container each label moves above its selects, which never shrink below their value. The date picker's time row now uses it.
