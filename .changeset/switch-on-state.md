`Switch` draws on as a green track and off as a grey track, both under a white thumb, from the new `--switch-track`, `--switch-track-checked` and `--switch-thumb` theme tokens (`bg-switch-track*`, `bg-switch-thumb`). Each track stands at least 3:1 against the page, cards and popovers in every palette. `SwitchFace` and the native `Switch` use the same tokens.
