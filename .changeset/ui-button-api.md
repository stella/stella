Button gains `variant="plain"` (a box-less click target whose look is its content, keeping the disabled disposition, tooltip and focus ring), `size="inline"` (an action inside a line of text), a `selected` prop with a selected treatment distinct from hover in every variant, and `focusRing="inside"` for targets inside clipping containers. Hover no longer paints a selected button.
