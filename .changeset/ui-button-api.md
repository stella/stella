Button gains `variant="plain"` (a box-less click target whose look is its content, keeping the disabled disposition, tooltip and focus ring, with `shape` for the ring's outline), `variant="muted-destructive"`, `size="inline"` (an action inside a line of text; an underlined link when no variant is given), `size="icon-2xs"`, a `selected` prop with a selected treatment distinct from hover in every variant, and `focusRing="inside"` for targets inside clipping containers. Hover no longer paints a selected button. Accordion gains `size="sm"` and `AccordionTrigger variant="muted"`, Label gains `variant="choice"` and `"choice-card"`, and DetailsGrid gains `variant="list"` and `orientation`. DESIGN.md lists one name per concept.
