`DatePickerPopover` gains localizable accessible labels (`dialogLabel`, `previousMonthLabel`, `nextMonthLabel`, `previousYearLabel`, `nextYearLabel`, `previousDecadeLabel`, `nextDecadeLabel`, `timeLabel`), `disabled`, `className`, `hideClear`, a bordered `variant="field"` trigger, `size="touch"` for 44px cells under a coarse pointer, and `mode="date-time"`, which reads and writes a zone-less `YYYY-MM-DDTHH:mm` value. Defaults keep the existing behaviour.
