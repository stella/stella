Table heads and cells take `align` (`start` | `center` | `end`) and `numeric` (tabular numerals, set to the end unless `align` says otherwise), plus `tone`, `truncate` and `flush` cells, a card `inset`, `scroll="parent"` and sticky or muted headers. `SortableHead` sorts through a muted `Button`, so it gets the design system's hover, focus ring and touch target; in a numeric column its arrow sits before the label.
