Add `sharepoint.sites.list`, `sharepoint.drives.list` and `sharepoint.drives.items.list` to browse SharePoint sites, document libraries, OneDrive and folders with your own connected Microsoft account, and `sharepoint.imports.create` to copy chosen files into a matter as documents. Importing a file again adds a version only when its content changed. They are available when the deployment enables SharePoint and your organization has turned the connection on.
