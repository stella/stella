Add `TextMark` and `textMarkClass` (`@stll/ui/text-mark`): one inline mark for words in running text, with `fill`, `underline`, `dotted`, `wavy` and `strike` variants, semantic and option-palette tones, and `rest`, `matched` and `active` states. `SEARCH_HIT_MARK`, `SEARCH_HIT_DESCENDANT_MARK_CLASS` and `textMarkHighlightRule` carry the search-hit mark to pre-highlighted markup and the CSS Custom Highlight API.
