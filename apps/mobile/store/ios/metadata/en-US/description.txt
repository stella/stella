stella is the open-source legal workspace for matters, documents, review and AI-assisted legal work. The app brings your firm's stella to your iPhone and iPad.

ASK STELLA
Chat with stella about a matter or about the law. Answers draw on your matter's documents and on case law, and every conversation stays in its matter.

YOUR MATTERS
Browse your matters by client, open their documents and items, and add a task or a scan from wherever you are.

SCAN PAPER INTO A MATTER
Point the camera at a letter or a filing. stella straightens the pages, saves them as a PDF and files it in the matter you choose.

TASKS
See what is due today, this week and later, add a task to a matter, and mark it done.

TIME TRACKING
Start a timer as you begin, pause it for a call, and save it as a time entry when you are done. A running timer stays on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

CASE LAW
Read decisions in a reader made for long legal texts.

MADE FOR IPAD
On iPad, your list and what you opened sit side by side. With a keyboard, ⌘K opens a command palette, and shortcuts move between tabs.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
stella keeps no analytics and tracks no one. Notifications say only that something happened, never a client's or a matter's name.

stella requires an account with a stella workspace. Sign in with your work email, Apple, Google or Microsoft.
