The black PAY by square print artwork in `pay-by-square.svg` comes from the
Slovak Banking Association's [official artwork archive](https://www.sbaonline.sk/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/Logo_PayBySquare.zip),
`logo_svg/logo-vertical.svg` (groups `g3522-3`, `g3493-4`, `g3487-0`).
The paths, colors, and proportions are unchanged; only the surrounding sheet's
other variants are excluded.

Use follows the [PAY by square logo manual 1.0.4](https://www.sbaonline.sk/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/pay-by-square-logo-manual-1_0_4.pdf)
and the [standard's terms of use](https://www.sbaonline.sk/wp-content/uploads/2020/03/podmienky-pouzitia-pay-by-square.pdf).
The 64 × 75 view box is rendered 37.254 mm wide, with the payment QR and its
four-module quiet area inside the 62 × 62 inset. This preserves the official
frame, complete wordmark, and recommended print size.
