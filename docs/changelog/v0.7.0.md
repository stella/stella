# Playbooks

## Turn your contract standards into reusable review checklists

Define acceptable, fallback, and unacceptable positions; capture preferred
language and negotiation guidance; extract key terms automatically; and set
exact rules for measurable thresholds.

Run a playbook across a table of documents or upload a file for direct review.
Compare documents with references, apply a playbook, or combine both. Share
playbooks with your team, refine them together, and approve versioned definitions
to keep reviews consistent.
