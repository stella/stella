# Maintenance release

stella includes reliability and maintenance improvements.

## Packages

- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): Include the pending Brazil jurisdiction in generated CLI contract schemas.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `conflict-checks.list`, `.get`, `.create`, `.review` and `.link` run and record internal conflict-of-interest checks where a deployment enables them.
- [@stll/ui](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/ui/CHANGELOG.md): `DocumentIdentityBadge` statute and case-number labels are named by their full form (`role="img"`, `aria-label`), so assistive technology reads `262/2006` even when a narrow slot shows `262/06`.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `resolve_law_citation` resolves article citations (`čl. 38`, `článok 46`) to their article, and a bare number to the article when the act has no section of that number.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): Add `lists.verifications.claim-rechecks.create`: checks one verified claim again against the list's current facts and records the new grade as a review event, leaving the run's claim unchanged. Claim reviews accept `fact-link` to link or unlink a fact of the verified list, and the review history lists links and re-checks.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `lists.verifications.get` returns `carryOver`: what a verification took from the previous verification of the same document against the same list. Notes and fact links follow a claim with the same words or with edited words in the same paragraph or page; other reviewer decisions follow only the same words with an unchanged verdict. It counts the reviewed claims that came across whole and lists the rest as `text-changed`, `verdict-changed`, `unmatched` or `ambiguous`. `lists.verifications.claim-reviews.list` entries carry `carriedFrom`, the earlier verification an action was first taken on.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): Add the `data-exports` capabilities: start an organization data export, list and read exports, and get download links for their parts and manifest.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): Clear law history for the selected country.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): Add `sharepoint.sites.list`, `sharepoint.drives.list` and `sharepoint.drives.items.list` to browse SharePoint sites, document libraries, OneDrive and folders with your own connected Microsoft account, and `sharepoint.imports.create` to copy chosen files into a matter as documents. Importing a file again adds a version only when its content changed. They are available when the deployment enables SharePoint and your organization has turned the connection on.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `skills.upload` and `uploads.create` (purpose `agent_skill`) accept skill packs up to 50 MB, up from 2 MB. A pack may hold up to 1,000 files, 50 MB uncompressed and 500 resources. `skills.upload` now also answers the installed skill's `name`.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `tasks.create` accepts an idempotency key: a retried create with the same key returns the first task or list item instead of creating another.
- [@stll/text-normalize](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/text-normalize/CHANGELOG.md): `@stll/text-normalize/hungarian-letters` adds `restoreHungarianLetters`, which reads the Latin-1 stand-ins "õ" and "û" (and their capitals) as the Hungarian "ő" and "ű".
- [@stll/ui](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/ui/CHANGELOG.md), [@stll/chat](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/chat/CHANGELOG.md), [@stll/workspace-ui](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/workspace-ui/CHANGELOG.md): Keep text that does not fit inside its container, and show the whole of it on hover, focus or tap only where it is actually cut.
- [@stll/ui](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.81/packages/ui/CHANGELOG.md): Add `ViewChromeBar`, the row that holds a view switcher and its `ViewToolbarChrome` on one line at every width. `ViewToolbarChrome` no longer wraps: it keeps the end of the row and scrolls horizontally once it outgrows two thirds of it.
