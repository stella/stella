# Maintenance release

## The case law corpus catches up, and switching organizations gets easier.

Decision ingestion is more reliable across the Czech and Slovak courts. Sources
that had fallen behind are current again, the Slovak Constitutional Court is
reachable after the court opened up how its search is accessed, and Slovak court
decisions now have their full text collected rather than case metadata alone.

Anyone who belongs to more than one organization can switch between them from
the sidebar user menu.

The rest is upkeep: the newest Claude models in the model catalogue, a round of
dependency updates, and a set of internal fixes and guards.
