# Austrian case law and configurable reports

## Search Austrian official decisions

Stella's legal-data pipeline now covers publicly enumerable Austrian decisions
from RIS and Findok. The sources include ordinary, constitutional,
administrative, specialist, historical, and fiscal court collections.

## Generate structured review reports

Configurable report specifications can produce DOCX reports with covers,
contents, findings, statistics, grouped contract sections, review matrices, and
appendices. Findings include severity, rationale, citations, and review status;
citation links open the relevant paragraph in the source document.
