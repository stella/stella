# Clipboard manager

## stella desktop now makes it easy to find and reuse what you’ve copied.

Press ⌘⇧V (Ctrl+Shift+V on Windows) in any app and your clipboard history opens in a glass panel over the document you are working in: text, formatted text, links and images, with app or website labels where available. Search as you type, arrow through the cards, press Return or ⌘1 to ⌘9 to copy one back, and the panel steps aside and hands the caret back to your document. Sort clips into groups in any colour, give them names, edit a clip before you paste it, pause capture while you copy something that should not be kept, and choose how long history stays. In 0.9.0, your clipboard history is stored locally and encrypted on your computer.
