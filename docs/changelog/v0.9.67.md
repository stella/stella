# Maintenance release

stella includes reliability and maintenance improvements.

## Packages

- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.67/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `case-law coverage` for a listed country that holds no case law yet (Austria, the United Kingdom, the United States) reports it as unavailable with the reason, instead of failing with "Case-law country not found".
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.67/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `organization-settings document-ocr-availability get` reports `type` (`live`, `batched` or `unconfigured`) in place of `available`, and a queued `entities ocr create` response carries the `availability` it was accepted under.
- [@stll/cli](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.67/packages/cli/CHANGELOG.md): `stella search-history list` accepts a country to list one jurisdiction's entries, and each entry names its jurisdiction.
- [@stll/ui](https://github.com/stella/stella/blob/v0.9.67/packages/ui/CHANGELOG.md): An error toast with only a title closes on its own after 8 seconds and shows the countdown line; an error with a description, rich content or an action still stays until dismissed.
