# Company registries in search

## Look up a company from the search bar, straight from the official register.

Type a company name into stella's search, open the Registries group and pick a country: stella queries the official business register on demand and shows a structured preview of the company, with identifiers, seat and status as the register reports them and a link back to the source entry.
