# Open WordPerfect files, e-invoices, certified emails and signed legal files

## WordPerfect and Apple documents, ZIP archives, iPhone photos, European e-invoices, PEC emails, signed containers and court dossiers now open like any other file.

<video controls src="https://stll.app/media/changelog/legal-file-formats.mp4"></video>

Drop one into a matter and stella shows what is inside: the documents themselves, their readable text and who signed them. Files inside other files open in place with their file type, the same way whether they came in an email, a ZIP archive, a certified message or a signed container. Search finds their text, and the AI reads them in chat and workflows just like a PDF. The original file always stays available to download.

- WordPerfect, Microsoft Works, Apple Pages and Numbers files, web archives (`.mht`) and e-books (`.epub`) preview like any other document.
- ZIP archives list every file inside, and each one opens in place, including emails and other archives.
- Outlook's `winmail.dat` gives up the attachments it hides, whether it arrives inside an email or on its own.
- iPhone HEIC and HEIF photos preview like any other image.
- Structured e-invoices (UBL and CII, including Peppol, XRechnung and ZUGFeRD, Italian FatturaPA and Czech ISDOC) read as a clear invoice: parties, lines, VAT and totals.
- Italian PEC certified emails show the envelope and every attachment.
- Signed `.p7m` (CAdES) files, ASiC-E, ASiC-S and Estonian BDOC containers and Slovak ZEP and XAdES_ZEP envelopes list each signed document by name, with its text.
- Czech data-box messages (`.zfo`) and Hungarian e-akta dossiers show the message and every document in it.
- Turkish UYAP `.udf` and Chinese OFD `.ofd` documents open as readable text.
- Images, plain text and CSV files preview faster, and text in older encodings reads correctly.
- Signer details are shown as recorded in the file, not verified.
