# Open signed and sealed legal files from more countries

## Signed P7M files, ASiC and BDOC containers, Turkish UYAP and Chinese OFD documents now open like any other file.

<video controls src="https://stll.app/media/changelog/legal-file-formats.mp4"></video>

Drop one into a matter and stella shows what is inside: the signed document itself, its readable text and who signed it. Search finds their text, and the AI reads them in chat and workflows just like a PDF. The original file always stays available to download.

- Italian signed `.p7m` (CAdES) files show the embedded PDF, including co-signed and nested signatures.
- ASiC-E, ASiC-S and Estonian BDOC containers list each document inside by name, with its text.
- Turkish UYAP `.udf` and Chinese OFD `.ofd` documents open as readable text.
- Together with Czech data-box messages (`.zfo`) from v0.9.87, which show the envelope and every attachment.
- Signer details are shown as recorded in the file, not verified.
